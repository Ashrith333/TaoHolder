How the network thinks

Bittensor is a market for machine intelligence. Each subnet runs its own incentive mechanism: miners produce, validators evaluate, and newly issued TAO follows measured performance.

  • Miners

    Serve inference, train models, and gather data. Rewarded on output quality as scored by validators.

  • Validators

    Run the subnet's evaluation, rank miners, and set the weights that distribute emissions.

  • Emissions

    New TAO flows to subnets in proportion to stake, then within each subnet in proportion to performance.

  • Stakers

    Under dynamic TAO, staking into a subnet buys its alpha token and raises its share of emissions. Capital allocation is the vote.

Participate

Three ways to hold a position in the network.

  1. Acquire

    Hold TAO in a self-custodied wallet.

  2. Stake

    Delegate to a validator, or stake into a subnet to receive its alpha token and a share of its emissions.

  3. Allocate

    Under dynamic TAO, stake distribution is the vote. Capital directed into a subnet raises its emissions; capital withdrawn lowers them.
